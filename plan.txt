2) The missing piece for Research Question A
To answer A, you need a student model + scheduling policy that uses attempts (mistakes) to decide what to ask next, and a controlled comparison against a non-adaptive policy.
Add these two modules:
A) Student Model
For each user and concept:
mastery score (0–1)
last_seen timestamp
history: correct/incorrect counts
“error type” counts (definition confusion, cause/effect, etc.) if you want
B) Scheduler (the “adaptive” brain)
Given the blueprint pool of questions, pick the next one by:
prioritizing low mastery concepts
spacing (don’t repeat too fast unless it was just missed)
optionally triggering a “repair set” after a miss (2–3 targeted questions)
Non-adaptive baseline: sample questions uniformly (or evenly across blueprint slots), no repair sets.
